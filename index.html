What we’ll cover

A practical session, not a product demo. You’ll leave with a few questions worth asking the next time AI comes up with a client, or inside your own agency.

  1. Why AI pilots stall

    The three places projects break down: adoption, governance, and fit. Plus a quick look at the “shadow AI” already running in most businesses.

  2. Two questions every AI program must answer

    “May this vendor touch our data?” is answered by a contract. “Can we prove our AI is controlled?” is answered by governance. Most companies confuse the two.

  3. Doing it right, in order

    Discover, correct, design, prove. Then how to run a pilot and a governance program side by side, and what the first 120 days can look like.

  4. What auditors and buyers will ask

    The questions showing up in audits and due diligence, results from client work, and a short self-score you can use on any business you know well.
